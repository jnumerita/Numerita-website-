How we sell

Payments

In the iPhone and Android apps, purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. In the web app, card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

Refunds

App Store and Google Play purchases are refunded through Apple or Google. For web purchases, email us with your receipt. We refund charges made in error and letters that did not unlock.

Cancel anytime

Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play settings, or in the web app under More, then Manage or cancel subscription.
